Piano samples
=============

Salamander Grand Piano v3
Recorded by Alexander Holm from a Yamaha C5, two AKG C414 microphones.

Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported licence:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Source:  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano
         https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3

Changes made: a sixteen-note subset (every minor third, C3 to A6) taken
from the web-optimised set published with Tone.js, trimmed to five
seconds with a short fade, and re-encoded for the browser.
